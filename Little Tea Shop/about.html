About Us

The Little Tea Shop was started in 2026 with a simple idea: to create a friendly place where people can enjoy delicious tea.

We prepare our tea using quality ingredients and traditional methods. Our goal is to make every cup fresh, flavorful and enjoyable.

Our Tea Story

Tea has been enjoyed by people around the world for centuries. At The Little Tea Shop, we bring different tea flavors together in one welcoming place.

From traditional Masala Tea to refreshing Green Tea, there is something for every tea lover.

Why Choose Us?

  • Freshly prepared tea
  • Quality ingredients
  • Affordable prices
  • Friendly service
  • Comfortable atmosphere
